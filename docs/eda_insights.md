# Exploratory Data Analysis & Business Insights Summary

## Executive Summary
Analysis of 7,043 customer accounts reveals that churn represents **$139,130.85 per month** in lost Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), which accounts for **30.50% of the entire portfolio's revenue**. Churn is heavily concentrated in identifiable segments that can be proactively targeted.

---

## Key Business Findings

### 1. High-Value Accounts Churn at Higher Rates
* **Finding**: The average monthly bill of churners is **$74.44**, compared to **$61.27** for retained customers.
* **Business Implication**: Churn is not driven by budget low-tier subscribers; rather, customers on premium plans (particularly Fiber Optic) are disproportionately defecting.

### 2. Contract Structure is the #1 Retention Lever
| Contract Type | Customer Count | Churn Rate | Monthly Revenue Lost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Month-to-month** | 3,875 | **42.71%** | **$120,847.10** |
| **One year** | 1,473 | 11.27% | $14,118.45 |
| **Two year** | 1,695 | **2.83%** | $4,165.30 |
* **Insight**: Month-to-month contracts account for **86.8% of all churned revenue**. Transitioning month-to-month customers to even a 1-year contract yields a ~74% reduction in churn probability.

### 3. The "Fiber Optic Dissatisfaction Paradox"
* **Finding**: Fiber Optic subscribers exhibit a **41.89% churn rate** despite paying the highest average monthly fee ($91.50/mo), compared to DSL subscribers at **18.96%** ($58.10/mo) and No Internet at **7.40%**.
* **Insight**: Premium pricing without adequate bundled support leads to rapid buyer remorse and defection.

### 4. Service Ecosystem & Support "Stickiness"
* Customers **without** TechSupport churn at **41.64%**, whereas customers **with** TechSupport churn at only **15.17%** (a 2.7x reduction).
* Customers **without** OnlineSecurity churn at **41.77%**, versus **14.61%** with OnlineSecurity.
* **Operational Recommendation**: Bundling Tech Support and Online Security at discounted rates on high-margin Fiber plans is a high-ROI retention action.

### 5. Critical Onboarding Window: First 6 Months
* **0–6 Months Tenure**: Churn rate is **52.94%** ($49,896.10 lost MRR).
* **49–72 Months Tenure**: Churn drops to **9.51%**.
* **Takeaway**: Customer retention is fundamentally won or lost in the first 180 days. Targeted onboarding check-ins and payment automation in months 1–3 will yield maximal CAC recovery.

### 6. Payment Channel Friction
* **Electronic Check**: **45.29% churn rate** (2,365 customers).
* **Automatic Credit Card / Bank Transfer**: **~15.2% - 16.7% churn rate**.
* **Takeaway**: Electronic check customers experience monthly manual billing friction. Incentivizing autopay enrollment creates substantial passive retention.
